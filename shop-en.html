Support the server

Sales only begin once the talks with Bohemia Interactive are finished. Here is what is planned and what you earn in the game.

  • Reserved spotPreview

    —Price TBA

    You secure a place in season 0. The whitelist runs in waves, and with the reservation you're sure to be in one of them. You start together with your wave, no head start comes with it.

  • SupporterPreview

    —Price TBA

    A Discord role, a name plate in chat and an entry on the credits wall for everyone who has carried the server from the start.

  • Clothing skinsPreview

    —Price TBA

    New colours and patterns for jackets, trousers and backpacks. Same stats, just a different look.

  • Weapon finishPreview

    —Price TBA

    Different wood, a different bluing, engraving on the rifle. It shoots exactly the same.

  • Base bannersPreview

    —Price TBA

    Flags and signs for your base, with your own crest. Pure decoration.

  • Your own master's markPreview

    —Price TBA

    As a master your goods carry your mark. With this package you design how it looks. The value of your goods stays as it is.

  • Queueunder review

    —Price TBA

    When the server is full you're let in ahead of the others. Only happens if Bohemia approves it, and only with no advantage in the game.

What you earn in the game

  • Weapons, ammo, vehicles
  • Materials, ingots, coins
  • Experience, skills, recipes
  • Bigger bases, more storage
  • Your start time is set by your wave

What always holds

  • If you buy nothing, you miss nothing in the game.
  • Every package is something you can see or something that keeps the server running.

How a purchase works

This is the plan. Nothing here is available to buy yet.

  1. 1

    Pick a package

    You choose a package and pay through a payment provider. Prices include taxes.

  2. 2

    Enter your Steam64 ID

    We need it so the package is tied to your account. The same ID as for the whitelist.

  3. 3

    Unlocked automatically

    A role in Discord and an entry on the server, without waiting for us.
