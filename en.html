Your house, your parlour

From the foundation stone to the pitched roof, you build with wood, clay and stone. Shutters, a fireplace and furniture turn a bare frame into a parlour, and furnishing it gives you comfort.

Wood rots and metal rusts, so you keep your base up with tar and paint. Fields, chickens, goats, a cistern and a root cellar: if you want, you live self-sufficient.

The island of Antoria

“In an emergency, the island provides for itself.”

That is what the civil defence leaflet says, and every new character carries it in their jacket. The harbour is abandoned, the south mild, the north cold.

The rest the island tells you itself, through things you find. What you discover goes into your trail book. Some places are dangerous for reasons you can understand, and whoever reads the signs gets further.

What's in

Season 0 starts as early access, and big updates follow. What comes next is always posted in Discord.

At launch

  • Four tiers, from stone to alloy steel
  • Base building with furniture, comfort and decay by material

  • Vehicles, raft and rowing boat

After that

Questions

When does season 0 start?

As soon as the date is fixed, it will be posted in Discord under announcements. Turn on the ping and you will not miss it.

Are there traders?

No. Everything that exists was made by a player, and trading happens between players: hand to hand, on the notice board or at a vending machine.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Every chain can be completed alone. A group just gets there faster.

What does the mod cost?

Nothing. If you want to help carry the server, there will be optional packages later that only affect looks. The shop preview lists what is planned.

Do I need the whitelist?

Yes, at launch. You can sign up in Discord.

Does it run on PlayStation or Xbox?

Sorry, no. DayZ mods are PC only.
