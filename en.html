ASHWRIGHT

A DayZ mod without NPC traders. You mine ore, smelt it in a furnace, forge your own tools and sell to other players what you don't need yourself.

Join the Discord Whitelist

What it is

I'm Raphi, and I'm building ASHWRIGHT with Pasi, who makes the 3D models. I've spent thousands of hours in survival, crafting and base-building games. Almost every one had something that was really good and something that got on my nerves. This mod brings the good parts together in DayZ.

You start on the coast with an emergency bag. After that it's about craft: find deposits, mine ore, smelt it into ingots in a furnace you built yourself, and forge tools at the anvil. A player who is good at this will be needed on the server.

What's different

  • No tradersNo NPC sells weapons or tools, and there is no starting money. A player made everything.
  • Quality has reasonsEvery ore deposit has its own grade, and it carries through to the finished item. How good it turns out also depends on the experience of whoever makes it.
  • 14 professionsMining, smelting, blacksmithing, gunsmithing, building and more. Every job earns experience in its profession.
  • Playable soloEvery chain can be completed alone. Trading saves you time, nothing more. Your level never locks a recipe.
  • Work is loudMining, forging and building can be heard from far away. So can the infected, and other players.
  • Talk first, shoot secondPvP with proximity chat. Outside the monuments the rule is: talk first, then shoot.
